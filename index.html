Solution architect professionally. Occasional programmer for fun.

I design systems for a living. Writing the application code is the part I do in my own time — deliberately, as a way of keeping my hands in the material rather than only drawing boxes around it. Most of what is here started as something I wanted to exist and could not buy.

I care about tools that carry load rather than hand it back to you, with a particular interest in accessibility and neurodiversity in software design.

What I'm building

NoteTaker

Android · Kotlin · coming to Google Play

Records a meeting, or takes an MP3, and returns you a transcript and a summary by email. Speaker separation, and a context field so the model knows whether it is sitting in an engineering review or a legal one. Everything is orchestrated on the handset: your own API key, your own mail account, no server of mine in the middle. Privacy policy.

md-docx-converter

Windows · open source

Bidirectional Markdown ↔ Word conversion, for moving text in and out of AI tools without it arriving mangled.

LightMD

Open source

A lightweight WYSIWYG Markdown editor, built for writing instruction documents for AI tools.
