Privacy Policy

NoteTaker for Android

Last updated 6 September 2026

NoteTaker records meetings, transcribes and summarises them, and emails the result to an address you choose. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes. The short version: the app runs entirely on your device using your own accounts, and the developer operates no server and receives none of your data.

Who is responsible

NoteTaker is developed and published by Chris Penner, an individual developer. Questions about this policy or about your data can be sent to financetoolcp@gmail.com.

What the app handles

  • Audio recordings. Captured from your device's microphone when you start a recording, or supplied by you as an MP3 file.
  • Transcripts and summaries. Generated from that audio.
  • Meeting titles and your "extra context" note, as you type them.
  • Configuration you enter: your OpenRouter API key, the destination email address, and your outgoing mail (SMTP) server details and password.

The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, contacts, location or device identifiers. It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

Where it is stored

Recordings, transcripts and notes are written to a folder on your own device that you select when you first use the app. They stay there until you delete them; the app does not remove them for you, and does not copy them anywhere else.

Your OpenRouter API key and your SMTP password are encrypted at rest using the Android Keystore, the operating system's hardware-backed credential store. Once entered they are never displayed back to you — the settings screen shows only whether a value is set.

What is sent off your device

Two things leave your phone, both initiated by the app on your behalf and both using credentials you supplied.

  • To OpenRouter. Your recording is split into chunks and sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to OpenRouter, which routes it to the transcription and summarisation models the app is configured to use. This request is authenticated with your API key and billed to your OpenRouter account. Your meeting title and extra-context note are included so the summary is relevant. OpenRouter's handling of this data, and that of the model providers it routes to, is governed by their own privacy policy and terms, not by this one. Please read them before recording anything sensitive.
  • To your mail provider. The finished notes and transcript are emailed to the address you configured, sent directly from your device through the SMTP server and account you specified. The message goes from you to you.

There is no intermediate server. The developer does not host a backend, does not proxy these requests, and has no technical means of accessing your recordings, transcripts, notes, credentials or email.

Recording other people

The app records whoever is present, and it is your responsibility to use it lawfully. In many jurisdictions recording a conversation requires the knowledge or consent of the other participants. Please obtain consent before recording, and be aware that meetings covering medical, legal, financial or otherwise confidential matters will be transmitted to a third-party AI provider as described above.

Permissions the app requests

  • Microphone — to record meetings. Used only while a recording is running.
  • Foreground service, including microphone — so a recording continues reliably when your screen is off or you switch apps.
  • Internet — to reach OpenRouter and your mail server.
  • Notifications — to show that a recording is in progress and to tell you when notes have been sent.

Children

NoteTaker is a tool for working adults and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect data from children — and, as described above, does not collect user data at all.

Your control over your data

Because everything is held on your own device and in your own accounts, you retain direct control. You can delete recordings, transcripts and notes from the folder you chose; clear your API key and mail credentials in the app's settings; delete your data at OpenRouter under their policy; and uninstall the app, which removes its stored settings. No request to the developer is required, and none can be actioned on your behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated date above. Material changes affecting how your data is handled will be noted in the app's release notes.
